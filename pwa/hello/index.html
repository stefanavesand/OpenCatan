Building costs and controls

Road: 1 brick + 1 lumber. Settlement: 1 brick + 1 lumber + 1 grain + 1 wool. City: 3 ore + 2 grain. Development card: 1 ore + 1 grain + 1 wool.

Select a build action, then tap a highlighted location. During setup, tap the highlighted intersections and adjacent roads directly. Bought development cards cannot be played until a later turn; at most one action card per turn.
